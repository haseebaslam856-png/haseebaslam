Everything you need to know about Local SEO and working with me.

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your online presence to attract more customers from relevant local searches. When someone searches "dentist near me" or "plumber in [your city]," Local SEO ensures YOUR business shows up — not your competitor's. 46% of all Google searches have local intent.

Most clients see noticeable improvements within 30-60 days, with significant results in 3-6 months. Unlike paid ads, Local SEO builds a compounding asset — the longer we optimize, the stronger your presence becomes. I'll set clear expectations during our initial consultation.

The free audit includes a complete analysis of your Google Business Profile, local citations check, competitor ranking analysis, on-page SEO review, and a custom action plan. You'll get a clear picture of where you stand and exactly what needs to be done to improve.

Regular SEO focuses on ranking nationally or globally. Local SEO targets customers in your specific geographic area. It involves Google Business Profile, local citations, map pack rankings, location-based keywords, and reputation management — all aimed at driving foot traffic and local calls.
